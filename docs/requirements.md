# Requirements

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| REQ-03 | Functional | Referral-only accommodation flow: no payments, contracts, or checkout; any in-app communication must remain optional and non-transactional. | Must | Matches acceptance criterion 3: "Referral only — optional in-app messages OK; no payments, contracts, or booking checkout." | docs/ACCEPTANCE.md — stakeholder statement: "Referral only — optional in-app messages OK; no payments, contracts, or booking checkout." |

| REQ-08 | Functional | Messaging: message threads are only allowed against listings that students are allowed to see. | Should | Matches acceptance criterion 10: "Optional message threads only against listings students are allowed to see." | docs/ACCEPTANCE.md — stakeholder statement: "Optional message threads only against listings students are allowed to see." |

## Assumptions

- No `docs/interviews/` directory or interview markdown files were present in the checked-out project, so the source references above cite the available public acceptance criteria file that explicitly states it is derived from Phase 1 stakeholder needs.
- The assignment notes identify listing lifecycle as the SPECIAL FEATURE, so those requirements are called out explicitly even though they are also covered in the public acceptance criteria.
- No additional product assumptions were introduced beyond the accepted stakeholder statements above.
